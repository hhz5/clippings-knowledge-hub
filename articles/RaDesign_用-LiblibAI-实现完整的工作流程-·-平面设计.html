Apply vibrant, high-intensity lighting in pink, blue, and violet tones that reflect boldly across the surface. Color reflections must appear bright and saturated, creating strong contrast against the metal. Shapes should be clean, playful, and slightly stylized. Introduce a few subtle frosted glass elements on secondary features of the icon. The frosted glass should have a soft translucency with a matte finish and slight diffusion of surrounding colors. Maintain a premium, collectible look without overpowering the metal finish. Avoid harsh shadows. The final result should feel futuristic and refined. The icon should be centered and floating on a solid black background. Square aspect ratio.

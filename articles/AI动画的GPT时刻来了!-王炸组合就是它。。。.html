我甚至找了悟空传里的几张插图，作为参考。

很快AI给我锁定 "Ink Wash" (水墨画) + "Vector-Noir" (矢量黑色电影) 。理由也很充分：

"Ink Wash" (参考你的图片): 对应你提供的插画风格，赋予画面东方的气韵、流动感和虚实结合的留白 。

"Vector-Noir" (参考《死神》风格建议): 既然歌曲是摇滚且带有《悟空传》的叛逆色彩，我们需要像久保带人（《死神》）那样“锐利、时尚的线条”和“高对比度的黑白” 。这能把传统水墨变得“硬核”，符合“金蝉子”不屈的灵魂。

然后生成短片 孙悟空大战奎木狼：

分镜一：云端的对峙 (The Celestial Standoff) Prompt Narrative:

A establishing shot in the style of 1961 animation Havoc in Heaven. The Yellow Robe Monster (Kuimu Wolf) stands atop a stylized, multi-colored cloud (Ruyi shape). He is wearing magnificent gold and green armor. The texture is flat gouache with iron-wire outlines.

Action: He strikes a theatrical pose (Liang Xiang), freezing for a second. His red beard flows rhythmically as if underwater, not blown by wind. The movement is snappy and energetic.

Camera: The camera performs a slow, flat Truck Left movement. No parallax issues. The background clouds move slower than the foreground (Multi-plane camera effect).

技术注解： 这里的关键是描述“beard flows rhythmically”（胡须有节奏地飘动）。在老动画中，毛发的飘动是逐帧画出来的，具有特定的循环规律，而不是物理模拟的随机飘动。

分镜二：变身与坠落 (Transformation and Descent) Prompt Narrative:

Medium shot. The Monster's face changes color from normal skin to Indigo Blue (Opera mask transformation). He spins around, transforming into a tornado of yellow smoke and ribbons.

Action: He leaps off the cloud. Instead of falling straight down, he performs a series of acrobatic somersaults in slow motion against a scrolling background of blue sky and abstract celestial architecture. The physics are weightless.

Camera: Vertical Pan Down following the character. The character remains in the center of the frame.

分镜三：落地的顿挫 (The Landing) Prompt Narrative:

Wide shot. A stylized forest landscape with flat, painted trees. The Yellow Robe Monster lands heavily in a "Horse Stance" (Ma Bu).

Effects: Upon landing, clouds of dust erupt, but the dust is drawn as curled ink lines and flat colored shapes, not realistic particles. He draws his sword, pointing it at the camera.

Lighting: Flat lighting, no dynamic shadows. The colors are vibrant and high contrast.

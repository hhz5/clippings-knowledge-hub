Prompt：**Primary Directive: Create a Minecraft 'Game Over' scene by replacing the person.**Your task is to edit the provided photo.

Follow these steps precisely.**Step 1: Replace and Repose the Person (The Most Critical Task)**- The original person in the photo MUST be **completely removed and replaced**.- In their place, add a new, photorealistic version of the same person.- This new version MUST be lying **face-down on the ground** in a prone position, as if they have just fallen or been defeated.- The pose should look natural and uncontrolled, like a video game 'ragdoll physics' effect.
严格按照以下步骤操作。**步骤 1：替换并重新摆放人物（最关键的任务）**- 照片中的原始人物必须**完全移除并替换**。- 在他们的位置上，添加同一个人的新的、逼真的版本。- 这个新版本必须**面朝下**躺在地上，呈俯卧姿势，就好像他们刚刚摔倒或被打败了一样。- 姿势应该看起来自然且不受控制，就像视频游戏中的“布娃娃物理”效果一样。
Their back should be facing the camera.**FAILURE CONDITION (Non-Negotiable):**- If the original person is still visible alongside the new one, the image is a FAILURE.- If the new person is not lying **face-down**, the image is a FAILURE.- This step is the most important part of the entire process.
他们的背应该面向镜头。**失败条件（不可协商）：**- 如果原来的人和新的人仍然可见，则图像失败。- 如果新的人没有**面朝下**躺着，则图像失败。- 这一步是整个过程中最重要的部分。
Do not skip or misinterpret it.**Step 2: Add the UI Overlay**- Once the person is correctly replaced and posed, overlay the classic Minecraft 'Game Over' UI in the upper half of the image.- This UI includes the text 'You Died!' in large, WHITE, pixelated font, and two gray buttons below it for 'Respawn' and 'Title Screen'.**Step 3: Add Dropped Items**- Finally, scatter several pixel-perfect Minecraft items on the ground around the fallen person.- These items must look exactly like they do in the game, not photorealistic renders.
请勿跳过或误解它。**步骤 2：添加 UI 覆盖**- 正确替换和摆好姿势后，在图像的上半部分覆盖经典的 Minecraft“游戏结束”UI。- 此 UI 包括大号、白色、像素化字体的文本“你死了！”，以及其下方的两个灰色按钮，分别用于“重生”和“标题屏幕”。**步骤 3：添加掉落的物品**- 最后，在倒下的人周围的地面上散布几个像素完美的 Minecraft 物品。- 这些物品必须看起来与游戏中的完全一样，而不是照片般逼真的渲染。

Prompt：**Primary Directive: Masterful Minecraft Scene Recreation**You are a Master Minecraft Builder. Your task is to look at the provided real-world image and recreate its background as a spectacular, sprawling Minecraft world.
提示：**主要指令：精湛的 Minecraft 场景重现**你是一位 Minecraft 建造大师。你的任务是查看提供的真实世界图像，并将其背景重现为一个壮观、庞大的 Minecraft 世界。
The person in the foreground must remain perfectly photorealistic.**Your Creative Process:****1. Inspiration Phase (Analyze the Macro-Structure):**- First, you MUST analyze the **large-scale structure** of the original background.
前景中的人必须保持完美的照片真实感。**您的创作过程：****1.灵感阶段（分析宏观结构）：**- 首先，您必须分析原始背景的**大规模结构**。
Identify the main landmarks and their general placement. Is there a mountain range on the horizon? A dense forest to the right? A prominent building in the center?- This analysis forms the **foundational blueprint** for your build.
确定主要地标及其大致位置。地平线上有山脉吗？右边有茂密的森林吗？中心有显眼的建筑吗？——这些分析构成了你的建筑的**基础蓝图**。
You are not copying the original 1:1, but you are capturing its core compositional elements.**2. Building Phase (Recreate and Enhance):**- Using the blueprint, construct a new Minecraft background.- The **major structures** you identified (mountains, buildings, etc.) MUST be built in roughly the same location and have a similar scale to their real-world counterparts.- For all other details (the specific types of trees, the ground cover, the rivers, the caves), you have creative freedom to build a beautiful and authentic Minecraft landscape that fits the scene.**ABSOLUTE AESTHETIC RULES (NON-NEGOTIABLE):**1.
您并不是在 1:1 复制原始内容，而是在捕捉其核心构图元素。**2. 构建阶段（重新创建和增强）：**- 使用蓝图构建新的 Minecraft 背景。- 您确定的**主要结构**（山脉、建筑物等）必须建在大致相同的位置，并且与现实世界中的结构具有相似的比例。- 对于所有其他细节（具体类型的树木、地面覆盖物、河流、洞穴），您可以自由发挥创意，构建适合场景的美丽而真实的 Minecraft 景观。**绝对美学规则（不可协商）：**1.
**Everything is Blocks:** The entire background, without exception, MUST be constructed from visible, sharp-edged cubes. This includes terrain, water, trees, clouds, and all structures.
**一切都是方块**：整个背景，无一例外，都必须由可见的、边缘锋利的方块构成。这包括地形、水、树木、云朵以及所有建筑物。

**对光滑表面或逼真形状零容忍。**2. **纯游戏风格**：最终背景必须与 Minecraft 游戏中的高分辨率屏幕截图完全一样。- **风格必须是**：块状、像素化，具有简单、明亮的灯光和硬边阴影。- **风格不能是**：照片级真实感、电影级或平滑的 3D 渲染。3. **仅限官方材料**：您只能使用 Minecraft 游戏中实际存在的块和材料。
Do not invent new ones.The final image must show a real person seamlessly integrated into a Minecraft world that is **structurally inspired by the original background** but **stylistically 100% pure Minecraft.**
不要发明新的。最终的图像必须显示一个真实的人无缝地融入 Minecraft 世界，该世界**在结构上受到原始背景的启发**，但**在风格上 100% 纯粹的 Minecraft**。
